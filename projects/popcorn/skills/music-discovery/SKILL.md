# Music discovery

Use MusicBrainz for artist identity and selected release discovery.

- Search for candidate artists, preserving MBIDs and source URLs.
- If search candidates are ambiguous, ask the user to choose before fetching details.
- For a selected artist, report MusicBrainz type, area, disambiguation and aliases when present.
- List the bounded release-group results with MusicBrainz links and dates when available.
- Describe only MusicBrainz-recorded data. Do not infer soundtrack relationships to movies, TV, anime or games from similar names.
- MusicBrainz results are community-maintained and may be incomplete.
